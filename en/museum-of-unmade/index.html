FAQ

What is this tool?

A playful generator: describe an impossible invention to get a museum exhibit card, or type your name for a nickname that changes every hour.

Is my text saved?

No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.

Are results random?

They derive from your text and the time, so the same text gives the same card, and nicknames change hourly.

How do I share my card?

Download it as an image or copy the link; your friend sees the same card.
