FAQ

How do I make an offers magazine?

Enter the title and store name, add products by hand or paste from Excel, upload product photos in bulk, pick a theme and size, then export PDF.

Which sizes are available?

A4 portrait and landscape, A5, square for social, 16:9 for screens and 9:16 story.

How many products per page?

From 1 to 16 per page, with pages created automatically.

Can I group products by category?

Yes. Add a category to each product and enable category headings so each group gets its own pages.

Are my photos uploaded?

No, everything runs in your browser.
