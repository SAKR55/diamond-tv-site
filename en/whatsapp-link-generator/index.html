How to use your link

Add the link to your Instagram bio, email signature, ads or website. When a customer taps it, a WhatsApp chat opens with your number and the message already typed, so nobody has to save or retype your number.

FAQ

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser; your number and message are never stored.

How do I enter the number?

Pick the country and type the number without the leading zero. You can also paste the full number with the international prefix.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. A wa.me link opens a chat with that number, with your message pre-filled, in both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business.

Why is the QR code missing?

If the message is very long the code cannot hold it. Shorten the message or leave it empty.

Where can I use the QR code?

On business cards, invoices, flyers and product packaging, so customers start a chat by scanning.
